# A Small Garden

A garden changes a little every day. Watch the leaves, check the soil, and keep a short record of what you notice.

## Looking Closely

Morning light makes small details easier to see. A new leaf may be bright green, while an older leaf feels firmer.

- Look before you water.
- Give each plant enough room to grow.

## Keeping Notes

Write down one observation each day. Over time, these notes tell a useful story about the garden.
